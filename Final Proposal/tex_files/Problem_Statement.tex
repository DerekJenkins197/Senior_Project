

A huge problem with firearms is that they are dangerous and can cause serious harm or death if not handled properly.
This is especially true when it comes to children getting ahold of firearms or unauthorized users who may not know how to handle a firearm safely. Police officers can
lose their firearms in the line of duty and if they are not recovered they can be used in crime. The proposed system, a smart trigger,
would help to address this problem by preventing the gun from firing unless it is used by the owner. 
Use of RFID technology to authenticate the user and ensure that only authorized users can fire the gun. 
This would help to prevent accidental shootings and unauthorized use of firearms, ultimately improving public safety.

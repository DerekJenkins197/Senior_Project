This project proposes an innovative solution to firearm safety. By utilizing near-field RFID technology, the system provides users with a secure firearm that remains immediately accessible when needed. We are developing a modular access-control solution for the AR-15 platform to enhance safety and prevent unauthorized use. Our primary goal is to promote the safe handling and operation of firearms by integrating a seamless, electronic authorization step into the gun's mechanical function. A fully functional prototype will be completed and rigorously tested for presentation at Senior Demo Day.
